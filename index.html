Application Point 03

How I Use AI Differently

Even though AI can not, and will never, replace lawyers and legal practitioners in analyzing legal issues, certain tasks AI handles can outperform tasks handled by a person, especially compilation and skimming tasks in legal work.

Application 01: Relevant Provision Compilation

When conducting legal research, one of the main risks, apart from misinterpreting a provision, is failing to identify all important provisions, and overlooks other potentially relevant provisions.

With a significant increase in number of legal documents in recent years, a lawyer not only needs (1) spending more time on analyzing unfamiliar provisions but also (2) on skimming and scanning relevant provisions to specific legal issues.

Application 01 is intended to skim through every single article of particular legal documents, then compile all potentially relevant provisions therein relevant to specific legal issues. The compilation will serve as a basis for lawyers' further assessment. This reduces the time required for (1), so that lawyers can focus more on (2).

For example of a practical case I used AI to assist the legal research as follows:

Background:

  • We have an agricultural land parcel (with LURC), currently classified as “đất trồng cây lâu năm”, with a total area of approx. 1 ha.
  • Current land use holder: Vietnamese citizen.
  • Land is already included in the master plan (quy hoạch) as rural residential land.

Request: Could you please help check quickly:

  • Whether it is possible to convert this agricultural land into residential land. We only need to convert 1,500-2,000 m² out of the 1 ha.
  • If conversion is possible:
    • What are the procedures and expected timeline?
    • What conversion fees apply, and what would be the estimated amount?
    • Any conditions or limitations should be aware of?

Application 02: Provision Status Scraping

  • For AI to perform concise legal compilation and skimming tasks as above, two essential elements are: (3) a clean legal document; and (4) a mapping that shows provision status, demonstrating which legal provisions have been amended, replaced, guided, or repealed.
  • The (3) is undifficult to provide. However, the (4) confronts obstacles.

Nowadays, many reliable legal webpages limit, even block, information that casual AI platforms (GPT, Gemini, and Claude) can access and extract by using an anti-AI web crawler and scraper, against "stealing" original content and restricting website visitors, Cloudflare. This phenomenon creates obstacles of scraping provision status, leading to incomplete legal research and hallucination risks if legal research is conducted solely using AI platforms.

A practical workaround is to use custom third-party code that interacts directly with the webpage and extracts information that is otherwise blocked or hidden, especially 6.
